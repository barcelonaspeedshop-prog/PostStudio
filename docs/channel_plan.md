## One warning first: what keeps us monetised

All four research reports flagged the same thing. Since July 2025 YouTube refuses to pay channels that look **mass-produced**: AI voice over stills, the same template every time, very high upload rates. It judges the whole channel, and a ban can spread to every channel the same owner runs. A wave of these channels was removed in January 2026.

AI narration is allowed. What protects us is what we already do, so we keep it strict:
- Every video has its own research, its own explainer and its own angle. Mike's approval of the script is our proof of human judgement.
- Vary the shape of the videos, not just the topic (story, explainer, countdown, quiz).
- AI clips only where they show what the narration says; never filler.
- Tick YouTube's "altered or synthetic" box on any video with realistic AI stills or clips.
- Human-sized upload rates: one long a week per channel, Shorts daily at most.
- Don't open channels just to try them (see the Omnira section).

## Gentlemen of Fuel: cars and car culture, past and present

**What young car fans are into (16–30):**
- **JDM and 90s/00s cars.** Hagerty's 2026 data on Gen Z's most-quoted cars: Honda Beat, Cobalt SS, Toyota Celsior, Acura RSX, Lancer Evo. The cars they grew up with are now rising fast in price (R33 GT-R, E46 M3, V10 M5, Golf VR6).
- **Games are the way in.** Forza Horizon 6 (set in Japan, May 2026) had 6 million players in 4 days. Gran Turismo made a generation want a Skyline.
- **Restomods** (old cars rebuilt with modern parts): 38% of owners under 45 pick one, against 22% of older owners.
- **Money worries:** UK insurance for 17–24s averages £1,150 and is rising (about £2,900 in London) while every other age group falls.
- The creators they follow (Supercar Blondie, ChrisFix, carwow, Donut, Mat Armstrong) all show their faces. **Our gap is the researched, explained story** that face-to-camera channels don't do.

**What to make:**
- Long videos alternate a classic story with a modern one.
- News Shorts on dated events we can prepare for (SEMA, auctions, reveals). We can't win the race to post first, so we explain why it matters within 24 hours.

## Omnira Football: keep the stories, add the modern game

**What works:**
- **Tifo Football is our model** (animated, narrated, 1.8M subscribers).
- Its biggest videos ask "Why…" or have one surprising angle: "Why Bayern never really loved Lewandowski" (1.9M), "Why are footballers addicted to snus?" (1.6M). Its top video is a quiz (2.1M).
- Its best videos are 3–4 years old and still earn. Evergreen explainers last.
- **Shorts without match footage:** stats and record graphics, quizzes, tactics boards, "did you know" history. Shorts pay very little ($0.05–0.15 per 1,000 views); they're for finding viewers. The long videos earn the money.

## The other Omnira channels

Recommendation: **don't open them now.** We can make about 12 long videos a month; every new channel takes videos away from the three that are working. Every extra channel also raises the "mass-produced" risk across all of them, and from February 2027 each monetised channel has to stay active on its own.
- **Food: skip.** Needs real footage of food and cooking; AI food stills look generic. The Gordon Ramsay story worked because it was really a business story.
- **Travel: skip.** Worst fit: it depends on footage of real places, and AI stills plus AI voice is exactly what YouTube is removing.
- **Cricket: skip.** Huge audience, but mostly in India and Pakistan, where adverts pay very little.
- **NFL: skip.** No UK edge; we'd be up against the whole US market on its home sport.
- **Golf: later.** An older, richer UK/US audience and good stories (The Open, the Ryder Cup, Van de Velde in 1999). Test two or three golf stories on Omnira Football first.

**What future channels should cover instead:**
1. **Motorsport beyond F1** (Le Mans, rally, MotoGP). Shares our research and graphics; MotoGP and endurance racing audiences are growing. Start as episodes on GoF and Omnira F1; no new channel needed.
2. **Aviation and engineering stories** (Concorde, Spitfire, Comet, Harrier, Vulcan). **The best candidate for a genuinely new channel:** a UK edge, masses of free public-domain photos, made for animated explainers, and advertisers pay well. Proven by Mustard (1.7M) and Real Engineering (5M).
3. **Business and brand stories** (car makers, restaurant empires, club money). The best-paying adverts of any subject we could cover. Test as GoF episodes first (Ferrari, Lamborghini, DeLorean).

Decide on any new channel after the first 90 days of numbers from the three we run.

## FB-CL1. Como v Manchester United

In 2017, Como weren't even a professional club. Tonight, they play Manchester United in the Champions League.

[On screen: SERIE D · 2017 · AMATEUR FOURTH TIER]

Como went bankrupt in 2004. And again in 2016. The club was refounded, and started again in Serie D, the amateur fourth tier of Italian football.

[Ladder graphic: SERIE D → SERIE C → SERIE B → SERIE A → CHAMPIONS LEAGUE, a dot climbing it with the years.]

In 2019, it was bought by the Hartono brothers, one of the richest families in Asia.

Then came Cesc Fàbregas. First as a player and part-owner, then as the manager.

In 2024, Como were back in Serie A for the first time in twenty-one years. And last season, they finished fourth.

So for the first time in its hundred and nineteen years, Como is in Europe. Straight into the Champions League.

[On screen: STADIO SINIGAGLIA · 13,602 · ON THE SHORE OF LAKE COMO]

Tonight, Manchester United come to a stadium on the shore of Lake Como that holds just thirteen and a half thousand people.

Can the fairytale beat United? Leave your prediction in the comments.

## FB-CL2. Bayern v Arsenal: Kane's old rivals

Nobody has scored more goals against Arsenal in the north London derby than Harry Kane.

[Counter: 0 to 14, a ball for each goal. On screen: 14 · NORTH LONDON DERBY RECORD]

Fourteen goals for Tottenham. A record.

Now he plays for Bayern Munich. And tonight, Arsenal come to Munich.

[Table graphic: ARSENAL 6 PTS (2 WINS) · BAYERN 4 PTS]

Arsenal have won both of their Champions League games so far. Bayern have four points.

[On screen: 2017 · 5–1 · 1–5 · 10–2]

The last time Arsenal played Bayern in a knockout tie, in 2017, they lost five-one in Munich. Then five-one again at home. Ten-two on aggregate.

And Kane is the favourite for the Ballon d'Or, which is handed out on Monday, in London.

A night against his old rivals, five days before the biggest award in football.

Who wins in Munich? Leave your prediction in the comments.

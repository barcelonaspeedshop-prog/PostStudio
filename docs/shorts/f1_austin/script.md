## Narration

Kimi Antonelli could be Formula One world champion this Sunday. Here's the maths.

[On screen: the points ladder. ANTONELLI 340, RUSSELL 238, HAMILTON 229.]

He's on three hundred and forty points. His Mercedes team-mate George Russell is on two hundred and thirty-eight. Lewis Hamilton, two hundred and twenty-nine.

After Austin, there are five races left. That's a hundred and twenty-five points, at most.

[The gap bar: 102 now, a line marked 125 to beat.]

So Antonelli has to leave Texas at least a hundred and twenty-five points clear. And there's only one way to do that: he has to win the race.

[Checklist, ticking in: WIN · RUSSELL 9th or lower · HAMILTON 5th or lower.]

Then Russell has to finish ninth or lower, and Hamilton fifth or lower.

Ninth would leave Russell exactly a hundred and twenty-five behind. Even if he won every race after that, they'd finish level, and Antonelli would take the title on race wins.

[On screen: YOUNGEST EVER · FIRST ITALIAN SINCE 1953.]

If it happens, he'll be the youngest champion in Formula One history, at twenty. And the first Italian champion since Alberto Ascari, in 1953.

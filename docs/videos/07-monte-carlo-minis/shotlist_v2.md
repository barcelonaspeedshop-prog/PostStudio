- ✅ already in media (commons / mike / ai)

## 8. How the lights worked (explainer, ~50 s)
- 🆕 `ai_minis_lamps_night.png`: the front of a works Mini at night with all its lamps blazing. This is the base for the explainer.
- 🎨 **Bulb A, the normal twin filament:** a glass bulb with two filaments. The main filament glows and its beam goes straight. Then the dipped filament glows, the beam drops, and an oncoming car's headlights appear. Tags: MAIN BEAM, DIPPED BEAM.
- 🎨 **Bulb B, quartz iodine:** a single filament, brighter (a glow bloom), one beam only. Tag: BRIGHTER, BUT IT CAN'T DIP.
- 🎨 **The switch:** a top-view road at night. An oncoming car appears, the Mini's main lamps go off and its other lamps come on. Tag: SWITCH LAMPS INSTEAD OF DIPPING.
- 🎨 Rule card: "STANDARD CARS: HEADLIGHTS MUST DIP AS BUILT", with a red ring on the Mini's lamps.
- 🎨 Quote card: BMC said the same bulbs had been used "for two years".
- 🎨 Two envelopes: FINAL RULES. The British copy shows "?" and the French copy shows "✓", captioned REPORTEDLY.

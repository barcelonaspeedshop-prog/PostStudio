## Description

January 1966. Three little red Minis finish first, second and third on the Monte Carlo Rally, the most famous motoring event in the world. Then the officials take the cars apart, for hours. And when they're done, all three are thrown out. Over their light bulbs.

This is the story of the Monte Carlo Minis:
- how a tiny family car beat V8s on ice
- the left-foot braking trick the Finns perfected
- the small print about lights
- the longest night in Monaco
- how a headlight dips, and why theirs couldn't

Was it a fair rule, applied to the letter? Or a way to hand the trophy to a French car? Tell me in the comments.

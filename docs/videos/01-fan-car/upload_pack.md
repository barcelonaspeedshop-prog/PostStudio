Title: BANNED: The F1 Car That Won Its Only Race

## Description

In 1978, Brabham found a loophole in Formula One's rules: a car with a giant fan on the back, officially "for cooling". It started third at the Swedish Grand Prix, won by 34.6 seconds, and never raced again.

Not because it broke the rules. Because it was too good.

This is BANNED: stories of the cars, players and tricks that were too fast, too clever, or too good to be allowed.

Should it have been banned? Tell me in the comments.

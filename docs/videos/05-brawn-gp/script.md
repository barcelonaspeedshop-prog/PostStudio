# IMPOSSIBLE: The F1 Team Bought for £1

## 1. Cold open  [0:00]

In December 2008, a Formula One team was told it had no future.

No owner. No money. No engine. Seven hundred people about to lose their jobs.

Less than a year later, that same team was champion of the world. Both titles. In its only season.

This is the story of Brawn GP.

---

## 2. The end  [0:30]

The team at Brackley had been many things. Tyrrell. BAR. And then Honda.

Honda had spent a fortune on it. And in 2008, the car was hopeless. Jenson Button scored just three points all year.

Then the financial crisis hit. And on the fifth of December, Honda pulled out of Formula One.

The factory was up for sale. Nobody wanted to buy it.

---

## 3. The gamble  [1:10]

But inside the factory, the team knew something nobody else did.

Honda had given up on 2008 early, and poured everything into the next year's car. And the new car was fast.

The team principal, Ross Brawn, refused to let it die. With chief executive Nick Fry, he took the team over himself.

The price? Reportedly, one pound. Honda helped with the bills for the first season, but the team had to cut hundreds of jobs just to survive.

There was another problem. The car had been designed for a Honda engine. Now it needed a Mercedes one, fitted in a matter of weeks.

On the sixth of March 2009, Brawn GP was born. The first race was twenty-three days away.

---

## 4. The secret  [2:20]

The car was almost completely white. There was hardly a sponsor on it.

But at the back, under the rear wing, it had something clever. A double diffuser. Two layers where the rules seemed to allow only one, sucking the car down onto the track.

Brawn weren't the only team to find the loophole. But they'd done it best.

The rival teams were furious. They protested. And in April, the sport's court of appeal ruled that the double diffuser was legal.

---

## 5. The impossible  [3:05]

Melbourne. The twenty-ninth of March, 2009. The team's first ever race.

Jenson Button put the car on pole position. Rubens Barrichello qualified second.

And on Sunday, they finished exactly where they started. First and second.

A team that hadn't existed a month earlier had just won its first race.

And Button kept winning. Malaysia. Bahrain. Spain. Monaco. Turkey. Six wins from the first seven races.

---

## 6. The fight  [3:50]

But the money was running out. Red Bull and the big teams kept developing their cars. Brawn couldn't keep up.

In the second half of the season, Button didn't win a single race. His lead shrank, race by race.

It all came down to Brazil. Button started fourteenth.

He fought his way through to fifth. It was enough.

Jenson Button was world champion. And on the same day, Brawn GP won the constructors' title.

---

## 7. What happened next  [4:45]

And then, Brawn GP was gone.

At the end of the year, Mercedes bought the team. The next season, the cars were silver.

That team went on to win eight constructors' championships in a row.

The team that was worth one pound became the most successful team of its generation.

And Brawn GP? One season. Two championships. A perfect record that can never be beaten.

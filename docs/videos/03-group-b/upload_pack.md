## Description

For four years in the 1980s, rallying had almost no rules. Build 200 road cars, and you could race a 500-horsepower monster down forest roads lined with fans close enough to touch it.

This was Group B: Audi's Quattro, Michèle Mouton, Peugeot's 205 T16, Lancia's Delta S4. And then, in the spring of 1986, it all went horribly wrong.

This is BANNED: the ideas that went too far, and the rules written to stop them.

Were the cars too fast, or were the fans too close? Tell me in the comments.

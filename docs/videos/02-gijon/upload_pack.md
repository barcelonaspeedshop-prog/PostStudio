## Description

Gijón, 25 June 1982. West Germany scored after ten minutes. Then, for eighty minutes, West Germany and Austria barely tried. Both teams knew 1–0 sent them both through, and Algeria home.

The crowd waved money at the players. A TV commentator told his viewers to switch off. And FIFA changed the rules of the World Cup forever.

This is BANNED: stories of the cars, players and tricks that were too fast, too clever, or too shameful to be allowed.

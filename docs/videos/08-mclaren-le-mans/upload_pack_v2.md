## Description

June 1995. A road car lines up for the Le Mans 24 Hours. Its designer never wanted it to race. It has never run for 24 hours, not even in testing. And it's up against prototypes built for one thing only: to win this race.

This is the story of the McLaren F1:
- Gordon Murray's three-seat idea and the gold-lined engine bay
- the grey test car No. 59
- sixteen hours of rain, and why aquaplaning hurt the light prototypes
- JJ Lehto's night
- the gearbox that had to last

Was it the car, Lehto in the rain, or was Le Mans just meant to go McLaren's way that year? Tell me in the comments.

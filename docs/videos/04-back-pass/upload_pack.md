## Description

Get one goal ahead. Pass it back to your keeper. He picks it up, bounces it, rolls it out... and you pass it straight back. Until the final whistle.

It was legal, it was boring, and after Italia 90, the dullest World Cup ever, football finally banned it. Then Denmark won Euro 92 with one last tournament of back-passes, and the rule change went on to invent the modern goalkeeper.

This is BANNED: the ideas that went too far, and the rules written to stop them.

Did the back-pass rule save football, or just make goalkeepers' lives a misery? Tell me in the comments.

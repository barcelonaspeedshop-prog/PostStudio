## Description

In the summer of 2015, the bookmakers made Leicester City 5000–1 to win the Premier League. The season before, they'd been bottom for months. Nine months later, they were champions of England.

This is the story of how Leicester did the impossible:
- what 5000–1 really means (some bookmakers offered 2000–1 on Elvis being found alive)
- the great escape, and the manager nobody wanted: Claudio Ranieri
- Vardy from non-league, Mahrez for £400,000, and N'Golo Kanté
- how they played: two banks of four, then straight for goal
- Vardy's 11 games in a row, the pizza and "dilly ding, dilly dong"
- the night it was decided, watched on a sofa at Jamie Vardy's house

Was it Ranieri, Kanté, Vardy's goals, or the season everyone else got it wrong? Tell me in the comments.

## Description

In 1963, Chrysler built fifty cars with jet engines and handed them to ordinary American families, for free, to use every day. 203 families drove them for three years and more than a million miles. Then Chrysler took almost all of them back, and destroyed them.

This is the story of the Chrysler Turbine Car:
- how a gas turbine drives a car: two turbines, one flame, and the spinning regenerator discs
- why it ran on diesel, kerosene, jet fuel… and, the story goes, tequila
- the bronze Ghia body, with jet-exhaust rear lights
- the 30,000 people who applied and the 203 who were chosen
- the throttle lag, the thirst and the high-altitude starts
- why 46 of the 55 were crushed or burned, and where the 9 survivors are now

Was it a brilliant idea killed too soon, or was Chrysler right? Tell me in the comments.
